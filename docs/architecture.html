Three-Phase Daily Greedy Policy Strict Execution Order

For every day $t \in [1, 90]$, events are processed in an exact, deterministic sequence that guarantees physical conservation of cash, stock, and debt:

1

Eligible Inflows (Cash & Materials)

Customer payments due today are collected; approved credit facility drawdowns deposit cash; raw material deliveries replenish inventory bins.

2

Neutral Operations (Production & Delivery)

Production jobs complete if required raw materials were consumed; finished goods are allocated to sales orders; orders deliver on time or log delay.

3

Cash-Consuming Commitments (Outflows)

Operational payroll, supplier invoices, and debt repayments are processed. If cash is below the bill, it is queued as late—cash never dips below ₹0.

Committed Cash vs Real Balance Deriving The "Red Dip"

Real balance stays non-negative because vendors simply aren't paid. The chart displays committed cash (what cash would be if every bill was paid on its contractual due date):

# Engine Derivation of the Red Dip: for day in range(1, 91): actual_cash[day] = run.daily_balances[day].cash # >= 0 overdue_amount = sum(b.unpaid_amount for b in late_bills if b.due_day <= day) committed_cash[day] = actual_cash[day] - overdue_amount if committed_cash[day] < safety_buffer: register_dip(day=day, deficit=safety_buffer - committed_cash[day])

The "Why the dip happens" inspector reports the causal chain (e.g., Sharma Yarn Mills bill on Day 41 delayed 7 days due to Kaveri Boutique receivable pending).

Empirical Delay Sampling (Monte Carlo) backend/app/services/risk.py

Rather than relying on abstract statistical distributions, Ripple samples payment delays directly from each customer's historical ledger behavior:

Empirical Shrinkage

If a customer has few historical invoices, their delay distribution is Bayesian-shrunk towards the global business customer average.

Worker Process Re-runs

The worker executes $N$ Monte Carlo iterations. In each iteration, sampled delays perturb the snapshot and re-run the pure deterministic core.

Read-Only Tool Calling

Financial Integer Invariants Zero Floating Point Math

To prevent IEEE 754 floating-point inaccuracies, every monetary figure in the database and engine is represented as integer paise:

# Exact Integer Conservation Invariant: closing_cash = ( opening_cash + sum(cash_inflows) - sum(cash_outflows) - sum(financing_fees) ) assert closing_cash == audit_ledger_total

Currency conversion to rupees ($\text{paise} / 100$) occurs exclusively at the browser edge in frontend/src/lib/format.ts.
